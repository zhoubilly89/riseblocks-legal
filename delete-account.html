Delete Your RiseBlocks Data

You can request deletion of your account or specific data at any time. Choose one of the options below.

Option 1: Delete My Account and All Data

🗑️ Full account deletion

This permanently removes your account, profile, sign-in identity, gameplay history, leaderboard entries, and any data linked to your user ID.

  1. Tap the email button below (or compose a new email manually).
  2. In the subject line, write: Account Deletion Request
  3. In the body, include the email address associated with your RiseBlocks account.
  4. Send the email. We'll confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete deletion within 30 days.
📧 Email Account Deletion Request

Option 2: Delete Specific Data Only (Keep Account)

📊 Data-only deletion

Use this if you'd like to remove certain data — for example your gameplay statistics or leaderboard entries — but keep your account active.

  1. Tap the email button below.
  2. In the subject line, write: Data Deletion Request
  3. In the body, list the data you want deleted (e.g. "leaderboard scores", "gameplay history").
  4. Include the email address associated with your account.
📧 Email Data Deletion Request

What Gets Deleted

Removed:

  • Account profile (email, name, photo URL, user ID)
  • Game progress synced to our servers (best scores, streaks, history)
  • Leaderboard entries
  • Pro subscription entitlement records linked to your user ID
  • Any pending support correspondence

Retained (where legally required):

  • Anonymized financial / transaction records (kept by Apple App Store and Google Play for tax and accounting purposes — we cannot delete these on their behalf)
  • Aggregated analytics that cannot be linked back to you personally

Note: Local game data stored only on your device (such as best scores cached locally) is removed automatically when you uninstall the app or clear its storage from your phone settings.

How Long Does It Take?

  • Acknowledgement: within 48 hours
  • Verification: we may ask you to confirm your identity by replying from the email associated with your account
  • Completion: within 30 days of verification

Cancelling Your Subscription First (Recommended)

If you have an active RiseBlocks Pro subscription, please cancel it before requesting account deletion. We can delete your account, but billing is managed by Apple / Google and continues unless you cancel through their systems:

  • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → RiseBlocks Pro → Cancel
  • Android: Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RiseBlocks Pro → Cancel
