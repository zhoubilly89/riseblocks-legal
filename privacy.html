Effective Date: May 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RiseBlocks ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application RiseBlocks (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you choose to sign in, we collect:

  • Email address (via Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple)
  • Display name and profile picture URL (from your Google or Apple account)
  • A unique account identifier (UID)

You may also use the App as a guest without providing any personal information, subject to certain feature limits.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

We automatically collect the following technical information when you use the App:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language and region settings
  • App interaction data: scores, games played, in-app navigation events
  • Advertising identifier (AAID on Android / IDFA on iOS), used by our ad partners
  • Crash and performance logs (where applicable)

1.3 Purchase Information

When you subscribe to RiseBlocks Pro:

  • Subscription transactions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • We receive a transaction identifier and subscription status from our purchase processor (RevenueCat) to grant entitlements.

1.4 Locally Stored Data

The following data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Best scores, streaks, and game progress
  • Settings and preferences
  • Guest play time counters

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core gameplay functionality
  • Authenticate your account and sync progress across devices
  • Process subscriptions and grant Pro entitlements
  • Display advertisements (for non-subscribed users)
  • Improve gameplay balance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
