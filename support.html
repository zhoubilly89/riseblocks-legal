Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

iOS: Open Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions, then tap RiseBlocks Pro and select Cancel.

Android: Open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, then tap RiseBlocks Pro and select Cancel.

You'll keep Pro features until the end of your current billing period.

I was charged but Pro features aren't unlocked.

Try the following in order:

  • Force-close the app and reopen it.
  • Make sure you're signed in with the same account used to purchase.
  • Email us at simbar3o7@gmail.com with your transaction ID (found in your Apple/Google purchase email).
Is my game progress synced across devices?

Yes — when you sign in with Google or Apple, your best score, streaks, and Pro entitlements sync automatically across all your devices.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at simbar3o7@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email associated with your account. We process verified requests within 30 days.

The game is crashing or running slowly.

Please try:

  • Updating to the latest version of RiseBlocks
  • Restarting your device
  • Reinstalling the app (your progress will sync if you're signed in)

If problems continue, email us with your device details and we'll investigate.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled directly by Apple and Google:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Refund Help Center
Is RiseBlocks available offline?

Yes — gameplay works fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for sign-in, leaderboards, ad delivery, and subscription verification.
